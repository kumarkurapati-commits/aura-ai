Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

AURA AI ("AURA", "we") is a free AI assistant. This page explains what information AURA uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google, or the name and email you type when signing up with email.
  • Your chats and projects. Your messages, AURA's replies, chat titles, project names and project instructions are saved to your account so you can see them on any device.
  • Settings. Your preferences, such as theme, the "About you" text and model choice.
  • Files you attach. Images and PDFs are sent to the AI to answer your question. We do not store the full files. We save only a small preview image and the file name with your chat.

How your information is used

  • To answer you. In cloud mode, your messages and attachments are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. AURA uses Google's free tier, under which Google may use what you send to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Please do not share passwords, financial details, health information or anything else sensitive.
  • Other AI models. If you pick a model from the "Other free models" group, your messages and images are sent through OpenRouter to that model's provider (for example NVIDIA or Thinking Machines). Free models on OpenRouter may log or use what you send to improve their models.
  • Web search. If you turn on Search, Google Search is used to find current information for your answer.
  • On-device mode. When the cloud is busy, AURA may run a smaller AI model inside your own browser. In that case your messages are processed on your device and are not sent to an AI provider.
  • Voice. The microphone and read-aloud features use your browser's built-in speech services. Depending on your browser, speech may be processed by your browser's provider (for example Apple or Google).

We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads.

Where it is stored

Accounts and chats are stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud). The website is hosted on Vercel. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

Shared chats

If you share a chat, anyone with the link can read that chat's text. You can stop sharing at any time from the chat's Share menu, and the link stops working.

Your choices

  • Rename or delete any chat or project at any time.
  • Delete all your chats, projects, shared links and settings from Settings → Delete all my data.
  • Sign out at any time from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

Children

AURA is not intended for children under 13. Children aged 13 to 17 should use AURA with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
